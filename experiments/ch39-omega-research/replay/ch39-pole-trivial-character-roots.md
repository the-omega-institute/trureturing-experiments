# Pole readout at zeros of the trivial-character minor

Use the nineteen-site five-charge model, common physical clock angles, and $0\le\delta\le10^{-8}$. The pole station is retained. Two missing ordinary stations belong to one cyclic orbit and the third to a second orbit. [Book research issue](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-books/issues/1784)

Let $A$ be the ordinary readout and $p$ the pole row. The full eighteen-station certificate gives $A$ full column rank. Choose a three-row orthonormal left annihilator of $A$, with one row per rotation character, and extend $p$ to a rotation-invariant row on ordinary coordinates by $b^*A=p$. Blind inputs then correspond to ordinary readouts supported on the three missing sites and annihilated by these four rows. In the orbit with two missing stations, write their amplitudes as their sum and difference. The two nontrivial rows eliminate the difference. The resulting $3\times2$ matrix has full column rank exactly when the layout is injective. Its first two rows give the trivial-character sufficient test. In the orbit basis this test is equivalent to invertibility of the five-by-five matrix consisting of the four unaffected orbit rows and the pole row. [Full eighteen-station certificate](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-experiments/blob/1c485355843e787bf64af78c6c21b0dffd0fbd78/experiments/ch39-omega-research/replay/ch39-five-charge-full-torus.md) · [Exact elimination](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-books/issues/1784#issuecomment-7237324584)

`verify` checks ten selected phase boxes, each with half-width $3\times10^{-8}$ in both angles. It holds the pivot normalization of each light line fixed. For any allowed $\delta$, the preconditioned zero map for the real and imaginary parts of the five-by-five determinant is a contraction taking the box into itself. The maximum verified ratio is below $0.4$. Each chosen orbit pair therefore has a certified branch where the trivial-character minor vanishes for the entire deficit interval.

For each box, `verify` also bounds the full sixteen-row readout for both assignments of the doubled orbit and all three relative offsets. At the center it verifies a numerical approximate left inverse using complex balls. Off center it bounds the Hamiltonian perturbation by the two clock degrees and the character-wise softening norms $4,1,1$. The gap $0.915$ converts residuals into errors of the projected trial columns. Subtracting the combined column error gives a normalized smallest singular value above $6.61\times10^{-5}$ in all 60 box and layout combinations. The full readout stays injective at the ten minor-zero branches. Uniform recovery over the remaining phase domain is open.
