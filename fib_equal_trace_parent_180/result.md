# Equal-first-trace certificate: result and boundary

The complete mathematical proof is §14 of the main repository's
docs/develop/theory/FIB_SELF_CALIBRATING_RELATION_MATHEMATICS_X.md.
Its source interfaces are VI Definitions 1.1, 8.1, 24.1, Theorems 8.2,
23.1–23.2, 24.5 and Corollary 24.6, at source revision
daec012452c47287e1a48afc68988b73e8ba9642.

For every nonzero real xi, every actual distinct MM collision pair with
equal first source trace satisfies F(theta,xi)=0. The primitive integer
F has total degree 20, degree 20 in each variable, 231 nonzero terms
and rational irreducible factor degrees 4, 8, 8. Its coefficient SHA-256 is
0fae329c1795f14dc7ce1374d5c67c4313a35229af9de7f2fc7ea0afcf7c4c51.

The arithmetic payload contains the original source-derived N,D, the
complete S=Res_b(N,D) and Z=Res_u(K,S), and the normalized
Z=c*xi^4*F. The determinant is -20*u*(u+1)*K(u);
Res_u(K,alpha)=1274500. The independently derived nonzero witness is
Z(0,1)=87 mod 103, with specialized degrees 3, 3, 55 preserved.
The compact exact-check result is same_trace_verification.json;
reproduction and relocation results are in experiment_result.json.

The zero-main-pivot chart is included by the prose proof's two simple
source roots, fixed-u implicit-function continuation, rank-two common-eta
identity, persistent Dk,Dq and original VI 23.2 continuous parent
reconstruction. Arithmetic checks do not replace that argument.

The rational fixed-parent comparison has image bidegrees (16,16) and
(18,16) and common singular candidate support 23b-29 up to scalar.
Its four fixed_0_*.json files retain exact inputs, images and supports.
These supports can contain roots at infinity or nonphysical sources and
give no global conclusion.

The full original strictly positive rank-one trace-one source domain,
nonzero real gain of degree at most three (all signs, degree drops,
sampled and repeated roots), arbitrary constant real drift, original
clocks, counted initial read, immediate reads, single unresettable and
uncopyable trajectory, and arbitrary deterministic adaptive/early-stop
quantifiers are retained. No condition on an independent MJ pair is added.

The unequal-first-trace incidence and global Bad nondominance remain
unresolved. An independent MJ pair still needs all three coordinate
difference charts and all original physical filters. VI 12.3–12.4 already
give actual failures and a varying bad curve; those are existing results.
There is no conclusion about the whole parameter plane, the other sixth
arm, root eight/nine-read recovery, optimality, noise or finite precision.
This package contains exact arithmetic evidence; mathematical review, Lean,
CI and delivery are separate.
