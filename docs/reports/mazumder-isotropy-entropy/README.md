# Mazumder Open Problem 9: entropy versus isotropy

Run `python3 check.py` with Python 3. The program uses integers and `fractions.Fraction`; no floating-point logarithms enter any check. Successful output ends with `ALL_CHECKS_OK`.

The fidelity check reproduces Appendix F of Arul Rhik Mazumder, *Exact T-counts of CCZ layers from an isotropy bound*, arXiv:2610.01024v2: the CCZ Choi-state fourth Pauli moment is 11/32, its order-two stabilizer Rényi entropy is 5 − log₂(11), the entropy bound is 4 and the isotropy floor is 7. It evaluates both the three-qubit phase-state and six-qubit Choi-state Pauli sums directly. It also compares the rank formula with direct Pauli sums for all 21 homogeneous cubics on zero through four variables and checks a seven-variable direct sum.

The 19-variable coefficient mask is specified in `check.py`, with least-significant bit first over lexicographically ordered triples. Two independent reconstructions, direction traversals and elimination procedures enumerate all 524288 directions and give ranks 0, 12, 14, 16, 18 with multiplicities 1, 31, 10970, 292882, 220404. Thus S = 457895/65536, the radical is zero, and the proposed entropy and isotropy bounds are respectively 40 and 39. The entropy comparison uses exact rational powers of 3/4. Block-diagonal slices give the tensor-power mechanism; powers 1 through 32 are checked as examples, while the all-positive-integer conclusion uses the algebraic argument printed by the program.

These computations support a preregistration and are not Lean certificates. A Lean implementation must prove the connection of the source's Choi/Pauli definitions to the slice formula and prove the binary elimination routine sound. It must then verify the complete finite certificate in the kernel without `native_decide`. The rank formula is prior work, including Kagamihara–Tsuchiya, arXiv:2602.23687; Chen–Yan–Zhou, Quantum 8, 1351 (2024), arXiv:2308.01886v2, already gives the random-cubic ensemble moment. Neither is claimed here as new.
