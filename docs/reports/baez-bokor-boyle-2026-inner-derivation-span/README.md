# Real inner derivation span of a Hermitian Jordan pair

The source is John C. Baez, Endre Bokor and Latham Boyle, *Jordan Pair Quantum Theory and the Standard Model*, [arXiv:2607.10833v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10833v1), Section 2.2, Proposition 8 and the unnumbered conjecture after equation (16).

Run from the experiments repository root:

```sh
python3 docs/reports/baez-bokor-boyle-2026-inner-derivation-span/check.py
```

Python 3 and SymPy are required. The stored run uses Python 3.14.8 and SymPy 1.14.0. All matrix entries, nullspaces and ranks are exact over the Gaussian rationals. Scalar absorption is also checked symbolically for `c = u + i v`, with real symbolic `u` and `v`.

The pair product is complex-trilinear, `t(x,y,z) = (xyz + zyx)/2`, rather than conjugate-linear in the middle slot. For `V_+ = M_{p,q}(C)` and `V_- = M_{q,p}(C)`, the code forms the full block-diagonal operator `delta(a,b)` with plus component `t(a,b,x)` and minus component `-t(b,a,y)`. It verifies outer symmetry and the Jordan derivation identity on every complex basis tuple, and product preservation by kappa. It reproduces Proposition 8's identity `kappa delta_v kappa = -delta_(kappa v)` and the fixed-point property of `D_v`, on all pairs of real basis vectors.

The code independently computes (1) the fixed subspace inside the complete complex span of the deltas, by nullspace equations for `sigma(T)-T`, and (2) the real span of every `D` on real basis pairs. Real bilinearity makes the latter a spanning family for all parameters in each tested model. The rank of the joined column spaces checks both containments, beyond equality of dimensions. It also checks the finite-sum averaging formula with non-real rational coefficients.

| Pair | Complex dimension of inn | Real fixed-space dimension | Real span of D |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| M(1,1) | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| M(1,2) | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| M(2,1) | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| M(2,2) | 7 | 7 | 7 |
| M(2,2), indefinite involution | 7 | 7 | 7 |
| Dual numbers C[e]/(e²) | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Zero-product pairs in dimensions 0, 1, 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The indefinite example is the Jordan algebra `M_2(C)^+` with product `(xy+yx)/2` and involution `kappa(x) = H x† H`, where `H = diag(1,-1)`. Its associated pair has the displayed trilinear product. The source's trace form is genuinely nonpositive: `Tr(z -> {E12,kappa(E12),z}) = -2`. The dual-number model has the nonzero nilpotent ideal generated by `e` and supplies a nonsemisimple check. Zero products include the zero-dimensional boundary. For the column models, dimensions 1 and 4 also agree with the source's description of the real inner algebra as u(1) and u(2).

The final output line is `ALL_CHECKS_OK`. These finite-dimensional model checks do not prove the unrestricted conjecture. A general averaging argument is required for the proposed positive settlement; no exhaustive positive enumeration is proposed as the main formal result.
