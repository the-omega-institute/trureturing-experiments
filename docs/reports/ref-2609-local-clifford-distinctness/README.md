# Local Clifford nonplussed distinctness

Run `python3 check.py` with Python 3.12 or newer and SymPy 1.14.0. The saved standard output is `output.txt`; successful execution ends with `ALL_CHECKS_OK` and exit 0. Every comparison uses exact integers, rational numbers or algebraic expressions. No floating point is evidence.

The source is Asad Raza, Jens Eisert and Bill Fefferman, *Distinctness threshold for pseudorandom unitaries*, arXiv:2609.03065v1, Proposition III.5 and the unnumbered question in Section I.C.a. The fidelity check reproduces the collision constant in Proposition III.5 by enumerating the 24 single-qubit Cliffords modulo phase. The six Pauli cosets are I, H, S, HS, SH and HSH; each is multiplied by X^a Z^b. The script checks 24 distinct phase classes, unitarity and closure under the generators H and S. It verifies that conjugating Z gives each signed Pauli four times, that the collision twirl is (I+SWAP)/3, and that the full two-copy twirl formula holds on all sixteen matrix units.

For n=1 through 4, the script constructs the exact tensor collision matrix, verifies its scaled-projector equation, an extremal eigenvector and the matching row-sum norm bound. It checks the commuting collision union inequality on every computational tuple for n=2 through 4 and 2 <= t <= min(4,2^(n-1)).

The settlement check computes the complete spectrum of the averaged true DNP projector at t=2 for n=2 through 5. On the distinct coordinates (x,y), DNP is the subspace of zero row and column sums. Independent constraint rows A give the orthogonal projector I-A^T(AA^T)^(-1)A. Traces against products of local swaps determine the local Clifford twirl through the exact inverse Gram matrix. Commutativity of these swaps gives all eigenvalues, so this checks every density matrix at these parameters. The minimum overlaps are respectively 0, 14/27, 398/567 and 22/27. The proposed epsilon is greater than 1 at n=2,3,4, making its concentration lower bound vacuous there. At n=5, epsilon=25349/30132 < 1 and the comparison is nonvacuous.

These finite checks support a preregistration of a general proof. They do not prove the assertion for arbitrary n and t, its negligible asymptotic rate, or cryptographic security. Only the tensor layer has depth one. The mathematical projector is onto Dist intersect NoPlus, never the product of its noncommuting projectors.

The geometric ingredients are Foxman, Lombardi, Ma, Nehoran and Wright, *Quantum Lazy Sampling and Path Recording for Any Group*, arXiv:2606.30281v2, Definitions 6.3, 7.2, 7.5, Lemmas 7.8 and 7.15, and Corollary 7.13. The source's PC security corollary assumes a global exact unitary 2-design, a hypothesis the local Clifford ensemble does not satisfy for n >= 2. This experiment makes no security claim from that corollary.
