# Aceska–Lort–Ripperger sparse 14-vertex DDMOG

`check.py` performs exact integer checks for the DDMOG in Figure 6 of arXiv:2606.16001v3 and for the nominated 23-edge counterexample. It checks the source's oriented-simple condition, the underlying graph's connectivity, the bijective labels `1,…,14` (vertices are their labels), and the incoming-minus-outgoing weight at every vertex. `output.txt` is the captured run; the final line is `ALL_CHECKS_OK`.
