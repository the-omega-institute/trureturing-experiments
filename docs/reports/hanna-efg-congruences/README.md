# Hanna derivative-substitution EGF congruences

Run `python3 check.py > output.txt` with Python 3. The checker uses only exact Python integers and `fractions.Fraction` from the standard library. It recomputes all four sequences for indices 0 through 300, prints every term, checks the two nominated congruences for each sequence, reproduces the live OEIS initial terms, and independently iterates each defining equation in rational formal series through degree 12. It also checks residual coefficients and denominator bounds used in the proposed proof outlines. Successful output ends in `ALL_CHECKS_OK`.

For A232694 and A232695, the large calculation uses the source's Lagrange-inversion identity. More generally, if B=A' and B=A(x B^k)^p, inversion gives E_n(B)=E_n(A^{p(kn+1)})/(kn+1). The exceptional case (k,p,n)=(-1,1,1) is computed directly from the equation. Coefficients of A^t use A(A^t)'=t A' A^t, with every integer division checked. The independent short calculation uses the literal differential equation, rational multiplication, composition, inversion and integration.

The nominated pairs are A232694: mod 3 from n=3 and mod 8 equal to 1; A232695: mod 5 equal to 1 and even from n=4; A232696: even from n=4 and divisible by 4 from n=10; A232686: even from n=2 and divisible by 4 from n=4. Additional conjectures in the entries are outside these checks. Finite checks support identification and the proposed mathematical arguments; they do not prove the universal claims. The residual sign is F' minus the substituted right-hand side.

Sources: [OEIS A232694](https://oeis.org/A232694), [A232695](https://oeis.org/A232695), [A232696](https://oeis.org/A232696), [A232686](https://oeis.org/A232686), Paul D. Hanna, 2013. Numerical source terms are quoted for fidelity under the [OEIS end-user agreement](https://oeis.org/LICENSE). This checker is original research code; it does not copy the source PARI implementations. The output contains the exact recomputed data.
